None of these palette options use red or green, so a series can never be mistaken for a critical or success status. Warning (yellow) and serious (orange) share hue families with some series, so the icon and label carry the meaning.

Usage rules

  • Assign slots in order, never cycle. Series 1 always gets slot 1. A ninth series becomes "Other" (grey/600, #8b8e89) or a small multiple, never a new hue.
  • Color follows the entity, not its rank. If a filter removes a series, the survivors keep their colors.
  • Scatter, bubble, maps, small multiples: any two marks can touch, so use only the three all-pairs-safe slots.
  • Two or more series always get a legend; four or fewer also get direct labels. Text stays in grey ink, never in the series color.
  • Separate fills with a 2px surface gap (stacked segments, adjacent bars). Lines are 2px; markers ≥ 8px.
  • Use chart chrome from grey: gridlines grey/200 (dark grey/900), baseline grey/300 (dark grey/800), axis labels grey/600.
